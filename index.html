    About COMFESTA

    COMFESTA 2K26 is the flagship technical symposium of the Department of Computer Science Engineering, built for students who think in code and build in the open. Across a single day, the department turns into a sandbox for innovation — where research meets rapid prototyping, algorithms meet artificial intelligence, and quiet coders get a stage. From high-stakes coding contests to AI-assisted builds and campus-wide puzzle hunts, COMFESTA is where technology meets talent, and collaboration outpaces competition.

    Rules & guidelines

    Please read carefully before you register

    👥 Eligibility & teams

    • Open to UG and PG students from all recognized engineering institutions.
    • College ID card is mandatory at the venue.
    • Team members may belong to the same college or different colleges.

    📋 Event conduct

    • Punctuality: Follow event timings strictly; latecomers may forfeit their slot.
    • Discipline: Maintain professional conduct and respect the judges' decisions.
    • Recognition: Certificates are issued to all registered participants.
